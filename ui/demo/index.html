Prototype boxes. Our anomaly detector decides when to flag a moment; for the first 2 seconds of each flagged stretch, a box marks the area of strongest movement as the stretch began, labelled with the whole-video class. The model does not pinpoint the crime itself, and the 2-second rule was chosen after viewing our example clips.

  • Every 5th frame is analysed (the project's sampling stride), with no cap: about 36 analysed frames per second on the GPU slice, so a 1-minute 30 fps clip takes roughly 10 s.
  • For a UCF-Crime video, only members that never trained on it are used, which is the same condition as the test score.
  • Strong on fire, explosions, road accidents and shoplifting; weak on Shooting, Assault, Fighting and Abuse, which look alike at 320×240.
